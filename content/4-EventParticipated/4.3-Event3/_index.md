## Presentation Overview

### Solution Development in a Hackathon

- Teams explained the product journey from identifying pain points and generating ideas to designing a solution and presenting a prototype.
- Practical experience in selecting a suitable technology stack for a specific problem.
- The importance of strategic planning and task allocation under strict delivery deadlines.

### Applying Cloud and AI to Product Development

- Examples of combining cloud infrastructure and AI to create scalable systems.
- Using managed Cloud and AI services to shorten development time and improve delivery efficiency.
- The foundational role of system architecture when moving a prototype toward a real product.

### Agentic AI and Intelligent Applications

- The potential of Agentic AI to automate complex processes.
- How AI agents can cooperate with external tools and services to build flexible workflows.
- The potential impact of AI on future software systems.

### Teamwork in a Hackathon Environment

- Team structures, task assignment, and coordination methods.
- The importance of communication, time management, and incident handling under schedule pressure.
- Using rapid prototyping to validate and refine ideas early.

## Personal Takeaways

- Understood the process of turning an idea into a feasible solution.
- Learned to analyze the problem before selecting technology.
- Developed a more user-centered mindset when designing products.

### Technical Knowledge

- Expanded my view of practical Cloud, AI, and Agentic AI applications.
- Learned more about flexible and scalable system architecture.
- Understood short development iterations: build, test, collect feedback, and improve.

### Soft Skills

- Improved my approach to discussing ideas and coordinating with a team.

## Proposed Applications

- Begin every technical project by identifying the user's core problem.
- Apply Agile or Scrum practices to short and time-constrained projects.
- Use Cloud and Agentic AI knowledge in future intelligent-application projects.
- Improve teamwork through clear communication and visible progress tracking.

## Event Experience

Attending **Hackathon Journey** provided a practical view of how a technology product can be created from an initial idea. The teams demonstrated that successful delivery requires the combination of technology, product thinking, and teamwork.

### Learning from Real Projects

- Followed different product journeys from problem identification to a completed prototype.
- Learned about technical obstacles and unexpected problems encountered by the teams.

### Exploring Emerging Technologies

- Expanded my understanding of Cloud, AI, and Agentic AI in software automation and user experiences.
- Gained a clearer view of how these technologies can work together in one system.

### Core Lessons

- Technology should solve the actual problem instead of being selected only because it is popular.
- Product success depends on technical foundations, product thinking, and team cooperation.
- Rapid prototyping is an effective way to validate assumptions and improve a product.
