- Understand recommendation problems and user feedback types.

- Understand cold start, filtering, and recommendation explanations.

## Week 4 Achievements

By the end of Week 4, the theory phase was complete and the project recommendation approach had been selected.

Key achievements included:

- Understood popularity, content-based, collaborative, and hybrid methods.
- Selected popularity for guests, content-based methods for new users, and ALS for returning users.
- Learned how click, watch, rating, and reaction events can become implicit scores.
